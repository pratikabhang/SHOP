About Us

Over Three Decades of Refreshing Taste Since 1991

Established in 1991, Dnyaneshwar Raswanti & Cold Drinks has been a trusted destination in Shrirampur for fresh and refreshing beverages. With more than three decades of experience, we have proudly served thousands of happy customers by maintaining consistent quality, hygiene, and great taste.

Our shop specializes in a variety of refreshing drinks including fresh sugarcane juice, fruit juices, cold drinks, lassi, tea, and ice cream. Every drink is prepared using fresh ingredients and served with care to ensure the best taste and satisfaction for our customers.

We are committed to maintaining high standards of cleanliness and hygiene in every step of our preparation. Our goal is to provide refreshing beverages that not only taste great but are also safe and hygienic for everyone.

Over the years, Dnyaneshwar Raswanti & Cold Drinks has become a well-known and trusted name in the local community. Our dedication to quality service and customer satisfaction continues to make us a favorite stop for people looking for cool, refreshing drinks.
